# Audiobook production plan (draft)

Publish audio **only from approved, versioned translation text**.

1. Export a fixed approved chapter revision and manifest.
2. Normalize punctuation, verse transitions, and pauses without rewriting meaning.
3. Review names, Greek terms, and liturgical pronunciation in a shared glossary.
4. Record human narration or synthesize using a voice with verifiable consent and reuse rights. Identify synthetic recordings as such.
5. Edit pacing, noise, loudness, silence, and verse/chapter boundaries.
6. Perform second-person listening review, spot-checking audio against approved text.
7. Deliver accessible chapter files, transcript, attribution, source/version metadata, and correction path.

Do not upload unapproved generated voices, borrowed performances, or copyrighted commercial audiobooks. An initial pilot should use Mark chapter 1 only after its editorial approval.

Source recordings and heavy model outputs can be stored separately from the Git repository, with checksums and manifest files referenced in releases. The choice of storage/distribution is an independent deployment decision.
