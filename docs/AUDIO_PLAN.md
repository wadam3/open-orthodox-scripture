# Audiobook production — working plan

Audio is one output of the Scripture project alongside **free ebooks/text and paid print editions**. The first translation pilot is **Genesis 1**, not Mark 1. **No audiobook is currently approved for publication.**

## Workflow

1. Complete the provisional AI-assisted Bible translation **before** inviting external collaborators, and clearly mark all interim audio proof-of-concept files non-release.
2. After qualified human Greek-language and Orthodox review of the relevant translation, freeze approved chapters to a precise release revision.
3. Plan narrator choices with written consent/voice-model reuse rights; disclose synthetic narration as applicable.
4. Create an Orthodox-aware pronunciation glossary for Greek names/terms, then script chapter transitions and pauses without changing meaning.
5. Produce and master chapter audio, preserving exact text revision, narrator/voice rights, model and generation settings where relevant, and verifiable source/version metadata.
6. Independently listen and compare approved text with narration; review level, noise, pacing, gaps, pronunciation, and accessibility.
7. Package **free** individual-book YouTube editions and explore **free** Spotify availability, subject to rights and service terms.
8. Package **paid** Audible editions as individual books, Old Testament, New Testament, and possibly an entire Bible when duration/metadata rules allow.
9. Prefer **one identical narration/master** for free and paid versions. A separate premium production is warranted only if meaningfully better; a different voice alone is not a requirement.
10. Publish required attribution and independent Church-status disclaimer with title/description/credits as supported.

## Distribution safeguard

Verify the **current** rules of Audible/ACX and any distributor **before** choosing exclusivity, AI narration, or commercial terms. Preserve nonexclusive distribution rights where possible to keep the same recording free on YouTube. Do not assume that Audible or Spotify will accept a given format or AI narration model.

Do not publish unreviewed Bible text as approved Scripture. Do not commit large audio files, copyrighted recordings, or unlicensed personal voice samples to Git. See [Publishing and distribution](PUBLISHING_DISTRIBUTION.md).
