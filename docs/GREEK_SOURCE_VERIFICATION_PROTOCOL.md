# Greek source verification protocol — photographic first-edition workflow

**Operational procedure v1.0 — 2026-11-12.** Applies to the provisional Orthodox Septuagint translation. First implementation: **Genesis 1**. This is a reproducible **procedure**, not a claim that all its checks have passed. Its governing project rules remain the [translation protocol](TRANSLATION_PROTOCOL.md).

## Governing source and evidence

- **Chosen primary text for Genesis:** H. B. Swete (editor), *The Old Testament in Greek According to the Septuagint*, Volume I, **1887 first edition**, printed Genesis 1 pp. **1–3**. Original historical catalog: https://archive.org/details/oldtestamentingr01swet ; alternate catalog and rights trail: https://commons.ptsem.edu/id/oldtestamentingr01swet .
- **Exact historical photographic page images logged for Genesis 1:** [printed p. 1](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0029=1.png), [printed p. 2](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0030=2.png), [printed p. 3](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0031=3.png), from Christian Classics Ethereal Library (CCEL). The 1887 *printed* page number is not the image-leaf number.
- **Page boundaries:** 1:1–13 on p. 1, 1:14 **crosses pp. 1–2**, 1:15–28 on p. 2, 1:29 **crosses pp. 2–3**, 1:30–31 on p. 3. The exact positioning of verse numerals and apparatus must be logged from the photographs rather than assumed from an electronic text.
- **Manuscript provenance:** Surviving Codex Vaticanus lacks Genesis's opening. Swete uses supplementary witnesses, including Alexandrinus (A). His **1887 printed** notes identify bracketed supplements within **1:20–25** and **1:29–2:3** where parts of A are lost. These are not claims that the whole verses are absent or that the other witnesses supplying each word have already been identified.
- **Not governing sources:** Later Swete printings, OCR/search snippets, third-party Greek digital text dumps, modern proprietary critical editions, and Brenton's 1844 *English* translation. Any such item may be documented as a separate comparator but must never silently substitute for 1887 first-edition Greek. Brenton is checked **after** original Greek work.

## A. Exact original-page transcription procedure (per verse)

1. **Pin the edition and the photograph.** Record source-register ID `SWETE-1887-V1`, actual full image URL, printed page, verse number, print/editorial witness label, access date, and repository commit. If the page cannot be opened or distinguished from another printing, mark the verse `blocked` rather than filling text from memory or an uncontrolled site.
2. **Inspect the print itself in reading order.** Locate the beginning/end of the verse; note line wraps, any split words, unusual capital letters and spelling. Where a verse crosses pages, inspect **both** pages. A first-pass paraphrase is not a transcription.
3. **Copy a *diplomatic* Greek working line** directly from the historical image, preserving every word, breath mark, accent, iota subscript, punctuation mark, capitalization, printed **square bracket `[` and `]`**, and genuine orthographic variation. Record any questionable mark as an unresolved item in the *research log*, not as a guessed character in an approved Greek source file. Keep the printed line/page mapping in separate metadata so that verse-level line joins do not lose provenance.
4. **Keep critical apparatus separate.** Transcribe any relevant note and source siglum in the apparatus log, linked to its exact verse/Greek span and printed page. Do not incorporate an apparatus annotation into Scripture text. `[` and `]` in Swete remain **part of the evidence** of the edited Greek: don't remove them merely to make text look cleaner.
5. **For damaged Alexandrinus text**, identify the precise start/end characters of each bracketed span even when brackets occur **inside a word**, span a line break, or start/end on different lines. Record `witness=A`, exactly what the *1887* apparatus says, and whether another witness providing the restored text has actually been confirmed. **Do not assert an unexamined manuscript source.** Flag unresolved spans `bracket-not-mapped`; never silently turn supplied wording into fully attested A text.
6. **Perform a separate character-by-character pass** against the original page (not merely the first transcription, memory, another model's output, or a later electronic edition). Compare letter, breathing, accent, case, punctuation, bracket, verse boundary, and page apparatus. Log checker/method/date and actual corrections; if both passes are AI-assisted, say **AI-assisted two-pass**, not independent human verification.
7. **Normalize only in a separate derivative** after the diplomatic source is checked. Document Unicode NFC, optional capitalization/punctuation normalization, and why a search/display copy differs from source print. Never silently “fix” rare Greek spelling to match a later standard edition.
8. **Record unresolved evidence before proceeding.** The chapter can be a provisional English draft with outstanding brackets, but it cannot be called source-verified until all verse-level issues are resolved or explicitly dispositioned under approved internal policy.

## B. Greek-to-English accuracy and narration check

For **every verse**, compare the source-checked Greek against our original English JSON with a fresh pass: full clauses, subjects and referents, negatives, tense/aspect, number, semantic categories, repeated phrases, verse boundaries, omissions, unwarranted additions, and intentional idioms. Log exact Greek and English spans for each discrepancy; classify as `source-error`, `translation-error`, `style-choice`, `variant`, `manuscript-supplement`, or `pending-verification`. Each decision has a stable ID, evidence URL/page, proposed fix, rationale, decision status, and English revision/commit. Verified substantive Greek or textual alternatives require accessible reader-facing notes; ordinary audiobook narration should not read apparatus or verse numbers by default.

For Genesis 1, the working issue IDs include **GEN-001-TRN-005** (“one day”), **GEN-001-ED-008** (second-day Greek goodness), **GEN-001-TRN-014** (governance clause restored from the 1887 printing), **GEN-001-SRC-002** (bracketed A losses in 1:20–25), and **GEN-001-SRC-003** (1:29–2:3). See the [chapter research](../notes/ot/GEN/001.md) and [historical-image first-pass report](../notes/ot/GEN/001.source-audit.md). No unrelated Hebrew comparison is required.

**Audio gate:** Generate the narration script **from the current English JSON**, compare its words mechanically to the verse strings, then listen for cadence, pronunciation, ambiguous referents and smooth day transitions. Record what was **actually listened to**, especially any revisions made after an earlier listening pass. “Good for audio” never overrides Greek fidelity.

## Contributor confidence scoring and independent verification — mandatory v1.0

This section is the **canonical, versioned** procedure for current and future chapters. See the [contributor's issue-to-PR workflow](CONTRIBUTOR_GREEK_VERIFICATION_WORKFLOW.md), [bracket rubric](GENESIS_BRACKET_CONFIDENCE_METHOD.md), [letter rubric](GENESIS_LETTER_CONFIDENCE_METHOD.md), and [automated confidence validator](../scripts/validate_greek_confidence.py). The **same scoring formula** must be used for each item. Scores express **editorial triage estimates**, **not calibrated probabilities** that a historical manuscript is correct. Consistent **arithmetic** is reproducible; any human assessment of how legible a printed mark appears is still an evidence-based but partly subjective input.

### Shared flags, states and provenance

- `flag_threshold_percent = 80`. **Strict rule:** `requires_rigorous_verification_later = (score < 80)`; **80 is not flagged**. For flagged items set `verification_queue = "deferred_rigorous_verification"`. Otherwise use `"routine_source_proofing"` for brackets or `"routine_1887_photo_proofing"` for letter-level verse readings. **Scores must not be inflated to clear a flag.**
- The state `verification_status` is **`pending`**, **`in_review`**, **`blocked`**, or **`verified`**. At any score, status remains `pending` until evidence shows actual new review. `in_review` means an uncompleted independent check; `blocked` records an illegible or disputed mark. `verified` is **item-level**, never chapter-level, and needs **two distinct reviewers' documented direct-image reviews**.
- Verification is independent of score. Preserve a below-80 numerical flag after an item is verified when its original diagnostic score remains low, but treat it as an **inactive** rigorous-review task because `verification_status = "verified"`. For every unverified item, even those **at or above 80%**, routine direct-photo accuracy checking remains required.
- Store a stable item ID, source edition, scan URL and printed-page/line locator, full proposed Greek reading, score and **all exact formula inputs**, narrative evidence, reviewer and issue/PR links where actually available. Record every revision in `review_history`. **Never invent a reviewer, source, PR, date or endorsement.**
- The ancient **1887 first-edition photograph** controls Genesis; a **1901-derived electronic text** is diagnostic only. Preserve third-party licensing and do not import the later transcription as the first-edition source.

### A. Bracket-boundary score — fixed algorithm

The bracket candidate is the precise printed **opening `[` / closing `]`** plus the proposed restored Greek characters between them, **not** the truth of the biblical account or English fidelity.

1. A reviewer assesses the **actual 1887 photograph**, records an explanation and assigns one **visual category**: `high` = **80** points (both bracket edges visually conspicuous), `medium` = **65** (plausible but some edge/letter ambiguity), `low` = **45** (substantial ambiguity), `unresolved` = **25** (boundaries unclear or a proposed span may be multiple brackets). This category is a **documented subjective observation**; independent reviewers may challenge the assigned category with new evidence.
2. Build `context = before + supplied_candidate + after` from the register. For the saved **working Greek at the item's verse**, or **both adjoining verses when the candidate crosses a verse boundary**, normalize each string by applying Unicode **NFD**, lowercasing, removing all Unicode combining marks, replacing final Greek sigma `ς` with `σ`, then removing every character except **`α` through `ω`**. Test whether the resulting full `context` string is a **contiguous substring** of the corresponding joined working Greek. If **yes**, add **5** points; if **no**, subtract **10**. This is *internal consistency* only, **not an independent source witness**.
3. If the candidate crosses a printed **verse boundary or a split line**, subtract **5** points **once**, even if both conditions are present; else add **0**.
4. If the proposed bracket **corrects or supersedes an earlier conflicting boundary interpretation**, subtract **10** points once, and cite the earlier proposal; else add **0**.
5. Compute **`raw = base + context_adjustment + wrap_adjustment + conflict_adjustment`**. Round to the **nearest multiple of 5, with exact halfway values rounded upward**; clamp to **20–90** inclusive. Because all current inputs are multiples of 5, no tie occurs in the current v1.0 data. Store `confidence_input`, `editorial_confidence_percent`, `confidence_reasoning`, source photo and `original_square_brackets_certified`.

**Worked examples:** a clear visually high bracket with matching context and no penalty gives `80 + 5 = 85%`; a previous contradictory boundary produces `80 + 5 - 10 = 75%` and **must be flagged**. A low visual candidate with matching context, a printed wrap and previous conflict scores `45 + 5 - 5 - 10 = 35%`. Later-edition agreement **adds no bonus** to a bracket score.

### B. Verse-level Greek base-letter score — fixed algorithm

This score triages a working **Greek letter sequence**, not individual accent correctness. The independent **1901-derived diagnostic comparator** is a different edition and cannot authorize a change to the 1887 text.

1. Obtain the saved working Greek verse and the separately identified diagnostic comparator for that **same chapter and verse**. If unavailable or incorrectly assigned (for example, the digitized `2:25`), record `diagnostic_comparator_available = false` and use **65 base points** without inventing a string match.
2. When both are present, normalize **both** via Unicode **NFD**, lowercase, remove Unicode combining marks, replace `ς` with `σ`, and strip all characters except **`α`–`ω`**. Count the remaining base letters `A` (working Greek) and `B` (diagnostic text). Compute a standard **Levenshtein edit distance `D`** with one cost per insertion, deletion or substitution, zero for matching letters; the inputs are the entire verse sequences, not handpicked words. Record `A`, `B`, `D` and `base_letter_agreement_percent = round_half_up(100 * (1 - D / max(A,B)), 2 decimal places)`. For an empty/unusable comparator use the unavailable case, not division by zero.
3. Base points are: `D = 0` **85**; otherwise rounded agreement **at least 99.00%** **80**; **98.00–98.99%** **75**; **95.00–97.99%** **70**; below **95.00%** **60**. When comparator unavailable use **65**.
4. Deduct **5** once if the verse crosses **two printed pages** of the chosen 1887 edition (e.g. `1:14`, `1:29`, `2:16`), otherwise **0**. This is computed from the recorded `printed_pages` array, **not** hand-entered independently. **`editorial_letter_confidence_percent = base - page_boundary_penalty`**. Enforce the shared strict `<80` flag.
5. Save evidence and score; **`independent_1887_character_certified` and `diacritics_and_punctuation_proofread` remain false** unless separate complete actual-photo checks meet the evidence requirements. Never describe a normalized later-edition letter match as certified 1887 accents, breathings or brackets.

**Worked examples:** exact letter agreement within one printed page gives **85%**; 98.84% agreement in one page gives **75%**, flagged; a missing/misassigned comparator gives **65%**, flagged; an exact match on a verse crossing two printed pages gives **80%**, *not* flagged. Any **meaning-changing or clearly photographed Greek error** must be corrected promptly regardless of a numerical confidence score; keep the old reading/score in revision history.

### C. Evidence requirements for future independent item closure

Use an issue and PR with [Greek verification template](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/greek_verification.md). To mark an item `verified`, provide a `review_evidence` object with **nonempty** `primary_reviewer`, `secondary_reviewer` (**different identities**), `primary_review_date` and `secondary_review_date` (real ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` dates), `independent_second_pass: true`, actual `original_image_urls`, an exact `printed_page_and_line` locator, actual `evidence_url` to the recorded issue/PR, `exact_printed_reading`, `apparatus_disposition` and `finding`. Include **`review_history`** with previous/proposed Greek, prior/new scores, reason, source and reviewer. In the same change set `original_square_brackets_certified = true` for the **verified bracket item**, or both `independent_1887_character_certified = true` and `diacritics_and_punctuation_proofread = true` for the **verified verse**. For unverified states these booleans stay false. A GitHub merge, a model's repeated reading or CI success is **not** an independent source reviewer.

Run `python scripts/validate_greek_confidence.py` and the other repository checks. The validator enforces **formula consistency, threshold flags, state/field consistency, evidence-field presence and a deterministic test fixture**; it cannot confirm that a claimed reviewer actually read the photograph. Explicit maintainer evidence review and genuinely independent source checking are still required. **One verified item never promotes a whole chapter to publication-approved or ecclesiastically endorsed status**.

**Policy versioning:** any future changes to the **numbers, normalization, inputs, rounding, threshold, or certification evidence** require a separately reviewed protocol version and migration note; do not silently rescore existing entries under altered rules. For later books, create book-specific registers while keeping these requirements; current CI is limited to Genesis 1–2.

### Mandatory verse-ledger fields

Stable ID; chosen printed-page image(s); original working-Greek file; `first-pass English meaning check`; `exact Greek diplomatic letter check`; `bracket/apparatus mapping`; `second Greek-English audit`; open issue ID(s); checker/date/evidence (or explicit `none`); outcome and git revision. **Statuses** are `not-started`, `working`, `blocked`, `checked-first-pass`, `checked-second-pass`, and `reviewed`. `checked-first-pass` does **not** mean certified. Only evidence-backed fields may advance. For future human work, record reviewer identity/qualification and consent only when actually supplied.

## D. Chapter completion / release barriers

An *internal* source-verified milestone requires (i) every verse faithfully transcribed against **1887 original** with edition-specific bracket spans and relevant apparatus, (ii) independently rechecked complete chapter, (iii) reconciled English, (iv) all source/translation discrepancies resolved or explicitly documented under project rules, (v) source provenance, legal intake and actual SHA-256 captured, (vi) machine structural validation, and (vii) a separate logged narration pass. If any gate fails, chapter stays provisional `draft` with the open items surfaced. **Machine checks and AI agreement are not a qualified human review.**

The later formal publication/approval gate is **separate**, to occur only after a provisional full-Bible draft under the project's sequence: qualified Greek-language, Orthodox, editorial and rights reviews; explicit sign-offs; versioned text, notes and audio metadata. No institutional Orthodox endorsement may be inferred from a research commit.
