# Publishing and distribution strategy — working plan

## Core goal

Make Orthodox Christian Scripture and literature freely accessible to everyone. Paid premium or convenient editions may support the work, **but no purchase is required to access the base translation**. Permitted commercial reuse by third parties is part of the proposed open-licensing model.

## Publication formats

| Channel / format | Intended offer | Access policy | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public project repository and downloadable ebook (EPUB and appropriate accessible formats) | Original reviewed translation, versioned by book and in larger collections | **Free** download, copy, adaptation, and commercial redistribution under adopted open license | Planned |
| Print-on-demand / print editions | Individual books, Old Testament, New Testament, and possibly complete Bible | **Paid** physical products; freely available text remains elsewhere | Planned; assess page-count, binding, cost, and quality |
| YouTube | Each individual biblical book, with chapter navigation, readable attribution and transcript | **Free to listen** | Planned |
| Spotify | Individual books/audio streaming or other eligible distribution format | **Potentially free** to listeners, subject to platform and licensing terms | Exploratory |
| Audible (and its eligible distribution channels) | Paid individual books, whole Old Testament, whole New Testament, and possibly a complete Bible | **Paid** retail audiobooks | Planned; verify platform requirements and duration/title limits |

## Audio master policy

- **Reuse the same narration and mastering by default** for YouTube and Audible. A different voice is not a reason on its own to create a separate paid edition.
- Produce a distinct premium version only when there are **meaningful enhancements**, such as improved mastering, navigation, contextual introductions, or responsibly reviewed supplemental material.
- Generate or record chapter masters once from the approved text and package into individual books, Testament collections, and larger sets if permitted.
- Include narrator/voice rights, AI-synthesis disclosure where applicable, pronunciation review, transcript spot-checking, version metadata, and rights evidence.
- Preserve **nonexclusive distribution** where possible so recordings can remain freely available on YouTube. **Do not sign an exclusive retail or distribution arrangement before confirming that free distribution and the proposed open license remain compatible.**
- Check current Audible/ACX rules, Spotify formats, free-channel requirements, runtime limits, file specifications, and any AI narration policies before production or launch. This document does not presume approval by a platform.

## Print and ebook plan

- Generate each book from the **same reviewed and versioned translation** used for audio.
- Publish an accessible free ebook/text edition, together with attribution and version information.
- Prepare print-ready single-book editions, two-Testament collections, and a complete Bible if physical page count and binding permit; multiple volumes may be necessary.
- Paid print packaging may include cover design, typesetting, navigation, paper, and production costs; the base Scripture text is still available free.
- Do not suggest any Orthodox church has endorsed a publication without a verifiable authorization.

## Preferred licensing and attribution

The proposed source license is **CC BY 4.0** for original translation and original audio, **MIT** for original software; legal/contributor policies are not yet finalized. Under CC BY 4.0, others can reproduce, adapt, and commercially sell compliant editions with appropriate credit and other license terms. Credit format can use **"Open Orthodox Library / Open Orthodox Scripture Project and contributors"**, a project URL, a link to CC BY 4.0, and a notice of modifications, subject to contributors' attribution requirements. Do not claim exclusive rights over original content that has been publicly licensed this way. Third-party materials carry their own verified restrictions.

## Independence and safety of claims

Use this disclaimer on the website/project pages and adapt it to print copyright pages, ebook front matter, and audio metadata:

> Open Orthodox Library is an independent publishing initiative. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or officially approved by the Eastern Orthodox Church, any Orthodox jurisdiction, diocese, or ecclesiastical authority. The term *Orthodox* describes the project's theological and editorial orientation and does not imply official Church recognition or approval. Translations are independently produced and should not be represented as officially authorized Orthodox biblical texts.

## Packaging and publication gates

1. Provisional AI-assisted draft of the entire intended Orthodox Bible.
2. **After the full draft**, qualified independent human language, Orthodox, and editorial review, with a traceable versioned approval record.
3. Text release plus free ebook formats.
4. Produce/master approved narration and prepare individual-book free YouTube audio, and consider Spotify if terms work.
5. Package paid Audible releases by individual book, Testament, and entire Bible where platform limits allow.
6. Publish paid print editions without restricting free access to the underlying translated text.

**Unresolved:** exact platform rules, production and distribution economics, qualified narration/voice licenses, branding/trademark clearance, print quality/cost, and a finalized rights and attribution policy. Nothing in this plan is a promise of platform acceptance.
