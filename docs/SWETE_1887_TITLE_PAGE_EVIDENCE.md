# Swete volume I — first-printing identity verified from the scanned title page

**Check date:** 2026-11-12, America/New_York. **Scope:** identity of the actual photographed book used for Genesis 1–2; **not** a statement that every Greek verse or manuscript bracket has passed diplomatic proofing.

## Direct primary printed evidence

The Christian Classics Ethereal Library (CCEL) [scanned preliminary page iii](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/Page_iii.html) links directly to the [photographed Swete volume-I title page](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0003=iii.png). That original title page visibly reads:

> THE OLD TESTAMENT IN GREEK / ACCORDING TO THE SEPTUAGINT / EDITED FOR THE SYNDICS OF THE UNIVERSITY PRESS / BY HENRY BARCLAY SWETE D.D. / VOL. I. / GENESIS—IV KINGS / CAMBRIDGE / AT THE UNIVERSITY PRESS / **1887**.

Adjacent [preliminary printed p. iv](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0004=iv.png) bears the Cambridge University Press printer's imprint. This establishes that the **same CCEL scanned sequence** containing printed Genesis [p. 1](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0029=1.png), [p. 2](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0030=2.png), [p. 3](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0031=3.png), and [p. 4](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0032=4.png) is **the 1887 printing**. The [scanned-page index](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/Page_Index.html) ties those leaves together in the same digitized work.

**Metadata caveat:** The [CCEL work overview](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1) contains a general summary mentioning “published in 1900,” apparently referring to a related Swete introduction or later context. For edition identification of **this physical image sequence**, the contemporaneous photographed **1887 title page** is controlling. Do not mislabel the first-edition page images as 1900/1901 merely because the website's description is broad.

**Independent corroboration:** [Internet Archive's 1887 volume-I catalog](https://archive.org/details/oldtestamentingr01swet) identifies the publisher as Cambridge University Press, year 1887, volume 1. It lists the historical item as `NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT` but that catalog flag alone is **not** an independent legal opinion or a rights sign-off.

## How to use this finding

- Greek editorial reconstruction and comparison should use the **1887 page images**, not generic online “Swete” transcriptions based on 1901 or other revised editions.
- This confirms **source edition identity**, not microscopic accuracy of accents/bracket positions in our verse-aligned working transcriptions.
- U.S. copyright status of the historical 1887 printing has a straightforward public-domain basis by publication age; other-territory, reproduction and downstream licensing questions remain separately tracked.
- The chapters' `source.transcription_verified=false` and `rights_reviewed=false` flags remain unchanged until their documented checks are truly satisfied.
