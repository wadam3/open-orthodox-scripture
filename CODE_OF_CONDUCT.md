# Community conduct

Treat contributors, reviewers, and readers with dignity. Discuss translation choices with evidence and precision; do not attack individuals over theology, identity, skill level, or affiliation. No harassment, threats, doxxing, or impersonation.

Assume good faith while maintaining high editorial standards. Disagreements about faithfulness should use documented source evidence and the defined editorial review process. Repository maintainers may moderate comments or restrict access to protect participants.

Report private concerns to a project steward through a contact channel to be published before contributor recruitment. Until then, do not include private personal information in a public GitHub issue.
