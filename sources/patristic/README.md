# Patristic original-language source workspace

**Status: structure only — no Apostolic Fathers' works, Greek transcriptions, or modern translations are imported.**

Reserved for independently verifiable, public-domain historical source texts of early Christian authors that help document how biblical passages were interpreted or used. Keep these texts **separate** from biblical source editions under \`sources/ot/\` and \`sources/nt/\`.

Read [the Apostolic Fathers primary-source policy](../../docs/APOSTOLIC_FATHERS_SOURCES.md) before importing any material. Preserve full historical provenance and rights decisions in the canonical [source register](../../docs/SOURCE_REGISTER.csv).

## Proposed future structure

\`\`\`text
sources/patristic/
  README.md
  <WORK_ID>/
    README.md                 # historical work, edition, witness, rights status
    passages/                 # original-language source excerpts after verification
\`\`\`

Only create actual text files after verifying: (1) specific public-domain historical edition, (2) the printed passage/page and its textual authenticity/recension, (3) original transcription verified against historical scans, (4) linkage to source-register rights record. This is an evidence workspace, not a separate biblical canon or publication of Church-approved commentary.

**No third-party license requests; no unverified modern electronic transcriptions, translations, or apparatus.**
