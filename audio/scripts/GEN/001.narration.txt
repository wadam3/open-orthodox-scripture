Genesis. Chapter one.

In the beginning God made heaven and earth. The earth was unseen and unformed, and darkness lay over the deep. And the Spirit of God was moving over the water. And God said, “Let there be light.” And there was light. God saw that the light was good, and God separated the light from the darkness. God called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. And evening came, and morning came: one day.

And God said, “Let there be an expanse in the midst of the water, separating water from water.” And it was so. God made the expanse and separated the water beneath it from the water above it. God called the expanse Heaven. And God saw that it was good. And evening came, and morning came: the second day.

And God said, “Let the water beneath heaven be gathered into one place, and let the dry ground appear.” And it was so. The waters beneath heaven gathered into their places, and the dry ground appeared. God called the dry ground Earth, and the gatherings of water he called Seas. And God saw that it was good. And God said, “Let the earth bring forth vegetation: plants bearing seed according to their kinds and likenesses, and fruit trees bearing fruit with seed in it, each according to its kind, upon the earth.” And it was so. The earth brought forth vegetation, plants bearing seed according to their kinds and likenesses, and trees bearing fruit with seed in it, each according to its kind. And God saw that it was good. And evening came, and morning came: the third day.

And God said, “Let lights come into being in the expanse of heaven to shine upon the earth and to separate day from night. Let them serve as signs, marking seasons, days, and years. Let them shine from the expanse of heaven upon the earth.” And it was so. God made the two great lights: the greater light to govern the day and the lesser light to govern the night, and the stars as well. God placed them in the expanse of heaven to shine upon the earth, to govern the day and the night, and to separate light from darkness. And God saw that it was good. And evening came, and morning came: the fourth day.

And God said, “Let the waters bring forth living creatures that move, and let birds fly over the earth across the expanse of heaven.” And it was so. God made the great sea creatures and every kind of living creature that moves, which the waters brought forth according to their kinds, and every kind of winged bird. And God saw that they were good. God blessed them, saying, “Increase and multiply, and fill the waters of the seas; and let the birds multiply upon the earth.” And evening came, and morning came: the fifth day.

And God said, “Let the earth bring forth living creatures according to their kinds: four-footed animals, creatures that creep, and wild beasts of the earth, each according to its kind.” And it was so. God made the wild beasts of the earth according to their kinds, the livestock according to their kinds, and every creature that creeps upon the earth according to its kind. And God saw that they were good. And God said, “Let us make humankind in our image and according to our likeness. Let them rule over the fish of the sea, the birds of heaven, the livestock, all the earth, and every creature that creeps upon the earth.” God made the human being; in God’s image he made him; male and female he made them. God blessed them and said, “Increase and multiply. Fill the earth and subdue it. Rule over the fish of the sea, the birds of heaven, all the livestock, all the earth, and every creature that creeps upon the earth.” And God said, “See, I have given you every seed-bearing plant that produces seed upon the whole earth, and every tree with fruit containing seed. These will be food for you. And to every wild beast of the earth, every bird of heaven, and every creature that creeps upon the earth and has life within it, I give every green plant for food.” And it was so. God saw everything he had made, and behold, it was very good. And evening came, and morning came: the sixth day.
