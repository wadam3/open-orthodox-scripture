# Old Testament — book workspaces

Proposed scope: **49 main books**, plus **4 Maccabees as an appendix** (50 workspaces). This is a working editorial inventory, not a claim of universal historical canonical enumeration or ecclesiastical endorsement.

Each book has a translation workspace and a separate research/translation-notes workspace. Existing Genesis 1 planning files remain in place.

**Approved drafting sequence:** Work through the books in the numbered order shown here, then 4 Maccabees as an appendix, and only then the 27 New Testament books. Finish each entire book to the provisional internal-review gate before beginning the next. The full list, detailed completion rules, and historical sources are in [the approved book order](../../docs/BOOK_ORDER_AND_WORKFLOW.md); [ancient alternatives](../../docs/ANCIENT_BOOK_ORDER_COMPARISON.md) explain where other manuscripts differ.

## Editorial mappings still to resolve

- Greek Septuagint book divisions can differ from modern English book names: 1–4 Kingdoms correspond to Samuel/Kings; Swete's Second Esdras spans material commonly divided as Ezra and Nehemiah.
- Psalm 151, the Prayer of Manasseh, and Greek additions to Esther and Daniel are in scope, but their final placement and numbering must be documented. No independent extra books are being silently added to the 49-book count.
- Source books and rights, chapter boundaries, Orthodox tradition variants, and the final editorial list require further verification before substantive translation.
- The 4 Maccabees workspace is intentionally marked Appendix. It is not part of the 49-book main list.

See [translation protocol](../../docs/TRANSLATION_PROTOCOL.md) and [Genesis source research](../../docs/SEPTUAGINT_SOURCE_DECISION.md).
