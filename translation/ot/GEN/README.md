# Genesis — first translation pilot

Genesis chapter 1 is the project's first planned **provisional** translation pilot, using a specifically selected and rights-reviewed Greek Septuagint edition.

Do not populate the source text or English draft until source provenance and reuse rights are documented. The English style/register has not been decided. Record exact source references, significant variants, AI use, translation choices, and unresolved questions.

The complete initial Bible translation is to be drafted before recruiting external contributors or reviewers. Do not mark a chapter \`approved\` until qualified human language and Orthodox theological review occurs in the later review phase.

See [translation protocol](../../../docs/TRANSLATION_PROTOCOL.md).
