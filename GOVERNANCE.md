# Governance (draft for adoption)

This community project has separate technical and editorial responsibilities.

## Roles

- **Project steward:** Coordinates scope, finances, public communication, and release decisions. Initially held by the project founder; formal assignment pending.
- **Repository maintainers:** Manage code, automation, formatting, security, pull requests, and issue triage. Orthodox affiliation is not required for technical stewardship.
- **Translation editors:** Review Greek source usage, English clarity, consistency, and documentary evidence.
- **Orthodox theological reviewers:** Qualified clergy or scholars identified publicly with their consent; assess theological and liturgical fidelity without implying their church's institutional endorsement.
- **Audio editors:** Review pronunciation, pacing, recording quality, and accessibility.

## Publishing authority

Maintainer merge access alone does not authorize a translation for publication. Approved chapters need a verified source-rights review, at least one language-editor sign-off, and the designated Orthodox-review sign-off. Reviewers may record minority opinions and unresolved variants.

## Transparency

Material corrections, reversions, conflicts of interest, textual variants, and editorial disputes are documented in public issues when appropriate. Private information and sensitive pastoral matters are not published.

## Open decisions

This governance document is a starting proposal. The owner must finalize appointments, appeal routes, accepted source editions, release signatures, and licensing before making a public translation release.
