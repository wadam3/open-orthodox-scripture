# Contributing

Thank you for helping build a careful, accessible, community-maintained Scripture project.

## Who can contribute

Contributors and repository maintainers do not have to belong to the same Church or share every publishing goal. Language, engineering, and editorial contributions are welcome on their merits. Final decisions about Orthodox theological fidelity require a separate qualified review process.

## Before submitting material

1. Open an issue describing the change, or claim an existing issue.
2. Record the exact source edition, bibliographic details, and rights status. Do not copy contemporary copyrighted Bible translations or audio into this repository.
3. Draft in a topic branch and open a pull request, with explanations for ambiguous Greek constructions, textual variants, proper names, and liturgical terms.
4. Disclose any AI-assisted translation or voice-generation use. AI output must undergo human review.
5. Provide the required editorial evidence. A merged technical pull request is **not** automatic theological approval.

## Proposed licensing terms

By submitting original work, you agree that it may be published under the project's chosen license: CC BY 4.0 for original text and original audio; MIT for code. Do not submit material you cannot license this way. Licensing policy is provisional until adopted by project governance.

## Review sequence

- Basic format and provenance checks
- Greek-language and English-language review
- Named qualified Orthodox reviewer approval for release
- Audio production and listening/QC review, when applicable

Only reviewed, release-approved chapters should flow into published audiobooks. Comments should be specific, evidence-based, and courteous.

## Pull requests

Keep changes focused. Use the pull request template. For Scripture text, include provenance, alternatives considered, and reviewer names (or leave fields pending). Do not mark your own translation as approved.
