# Genesis 1–2: ordinary Greek Unicode and source-proofing readiness

**Review date:** 2026-11-12, America/New_York. **Scope:** all **44 verses outside the noted Alexandrinus-restoration ranges**, plus a local Unicode sanity scan of the remaining 12 working Greek verses. This is **not** a visual image-to-letter or accent-by-accent proofread. It must not be represented as a complete Greek source accuracy check.

## Verified from the actual saved working files

- The repository contains **56** consecutively indexed, non-empty Greek working verse lines: Genesis 1:1–31 and Genesis 2:1–25.
- All **56** are internally valid Unicode text in **NFC**, contain Greek script, and have no replacement characters, ASCII control characters or Latin letters in the extracted verse line. These checks detect text-encoding corruption and mixed-script errors; they do **not** tell us if a Greek letter, accent, breathing, iota subscript or printed punctuation agrees with the **1887 photo**.
- **44** verses are outside the two ranges that Swete's original apparatus singles out for Alexandrinus editorial restorations: **Genesis 1:1–19, 1:26–28 and Genesis 2:4–25**. Their Greek **character proofing against the photo is still NOT performed**; absence of known damaged-witness brackets is not evidence of a source-level pass.
- The other **12** verses are in the bracketed ranges (**1:20–25, 1:29–31, 2:1–3**) and also pass the encoding checks. The bracket maps remain *provisional* and separate.
- For exact source photographs see [p. 1](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0029=1.png), [p. 2](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0030=2.png), [p. 3](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0031=3.png), and [p. 4](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0032=4.png). A page number identifies where to proofread; it is **not** evidence of an already-completed proofread.
