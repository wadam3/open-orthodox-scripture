## New line-by-line original-image check (2026-11-12)

**Primary evidence reopened in this pass:** Swete 1887 original photograph [printed p. 3](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0031=3.png), [scan viewer](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/Page_3.html). The physical page's title is **ΓΕΝΕΣΙΣ**, it displays Genesis 2 immediately after 1:31, and its bottom apparatus carries the Alexandrinus loss notice for `1:29–2:3`. This is a **new image-based check** rather than a copy from Perseus or any retyped edition.

- **2:1, two adjoining printed lines:** the print separates the source-supplied `[Καὶ]` before `συνετελέσθησαν`, `[ὁ οὐρανὸς καὶ]` after that verb, and `[αὐτῶν.]` at the close. The *three bracket openings are distinct*. The earliest bracket-map row's phrase “near `[Καὶ`” is superseded by the more precise isolated candidate `[Καὶ]`.
- **2:2:** the printed `[ἐν τῇ ἡμέρᾳ] τῇ ἕκτῃ` keeps **`ἕκτῃ` outside the first restoration**. More brackets continue later in the same verse after `ἃ ἐποίησ…`, around the seventh-day rest formula, and around `ἐποίησ…` again. The **end letters and their placement remain tentative at this image resolution**; earlier rows `[ἃ ἐποίησε]` and `[ἐποίησε]` are not enough to certify whole-word boundaries. Leave their full extents open.
- **2:3:** the photograph shows a distinct `[ὁ θεὸς τὴν]` after `καὶ ηὐλόγησεν`, then the blessing and sanctification clause continues outside that supplement. No reason to change the English “God blessed the seventh day,” since Swete's edited Greek contains that clause, but it requires a reader-facing explanatory note about the damaged A witness.
- **Printed page break:** 2:16 begins on p. 3 and is completed on [1887 p. 4](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0032=4.png). Direct check confirms the rest of 2:16 appears **before** the raised `17` verse marker on p. 4; assigning the opening of p. 4 exclusively to 2:17 is a source-map error.

**Result of this pass:** three separate restored pieces in **2:1** and the `2:2` sixth-day bracket boundary are now more explicitly documented as **AI-checked original-photo observations**. The verse isn't source-certified: the other 2:2 brackets and remaining 2:1–3 diacritics, the apparatus witness entries, and an independent check are not complete. **English and narration are unchanged.**

## English impact and disposition

The provisional English [Genesis 2:1–25](../../../translation/ot/GEN/002.json) currently reads **“God completed on the sixth day ... and on the seventh day he rested”** in 2:2, reflecting the selected first-edition edited Greek. This visual bracket mapping does **not** justify reverting the verse to a competing reading. The final edition should provide an accessible footnote explaining where Swete supplied text in missing portions of A without breaking the continuous audio narration.
