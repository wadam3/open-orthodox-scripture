This is a **second AI-assisted semantic / clause-coverage check**, **not** an independent human translation review or a character-by-character diplomatic transcription. An AI-only second look may confirm clauses and spot omissions; it does **not** satisfy the project's independent sign-off gate. Both source proofing and qualified human review remain open.

**Checks actually made:** read the draft's 25 verse strings with the visible original-page Greek and compare main clauses, participants, imperative/negative scope, repetitions, geography, verse boundaries and notable LXX-specific wording; preserve draft apparatus notes separately from the narration. A complete image-to-Unicode transcription preserving **every accent, breathing, square bracket, line wrap, and apparatus siglum** was **not** performed. Source-certification status must remain false. ## Wording corrections applied after the original 25-verse pilot

1. **2:9**: Original English “And God caused to grow…” silently omitted 1887 `ἔτι` after `ἐξανέτειλεν ὁ θεὸς`. The saved verse now reads **“And God also caused to grow…”**. This is a modest recovery of the Greek's “also/further”; it does not imply an external manuscript conclusion. Reader note `GEN-002-TRN-009`.
2. **2:19**: Original English “And God formed from the earth…” omitted the second `ἔτι`, printed `καὶ ἔπλασεν ὁ θεὸς ἔτι ἐκ τῆς γῆς`. The saved verse now reads **“And God also formed from the earth…”**. Reader note `GEN-002-TRN-019`. This is an English-clause correction only, **not** a verified manuscript reconstruction.

The selected text's **sixth-day completion** at **2:2** remains intact. The first-English-pilot correction in **2:1**, dropping unsupported “In the end,” also remains intact. Do not alter these to mimic English in another textual tradition.
