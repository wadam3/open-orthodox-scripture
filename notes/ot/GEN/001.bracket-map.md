# Genesis 1 — incomplete original-image bracket observations

**2026-11-12 — FIRST-PASS VISUAL RESEARCH ONLY. NOT VERIFIED OR DIPLOMATIC GREEK.** The passages noted here are from the photographic **1887 first printing** of H. B. Swete, *The Old Testament in Greek According to the Septuagint*, vol. I. This is **not a complete bracket map** and contains **tentative readings** whose letters, punctuation, diacritics and closing bracket locations require further enlarged-image and independent review. Do not promote this file to `sources/`, change chapter status, or represent these as an exact first-edition transcription.

**Direct source images:** [1887 printed page 2](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0030=2.png) (Genesis 1:14–29 begins); [1887 printed page 3](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0031=3.png) (Genesis 1:29 continues through Genesis 2). When direct binary URLs fail, use the [scanned-page index](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/Page_Index.html) and its linked image pages. The old printed p. 2 footnote discusses bracketed losses in Alexandrinus (A) at **1:20–25**; p. 3 discusses **1:29–2:3**. Neither notice means that every verse in these ranges is entirely absent from A.
