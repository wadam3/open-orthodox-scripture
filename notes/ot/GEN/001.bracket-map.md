### Targeted follow-up: verse 29–31 original-image check (2026-11-12)

**Source actually re-opened:** the photographed [1887 printed page 3](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0031=3.png), checked against its [scan landing page](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/Page_3.html), plus [printed page 2](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0030=2.png) for the opening of verse 29. This is a **second targeted AI-assisted visual look**, not independent human collation or certified character-level proofreading.

- **1:29 crossing into 1:30, printed p. 3, opening lines:** a square bracket opens before `ὑμῖν ἔσται εἰς` near the end of the second running-text line. The text continues onto the following line with `βρῶσιν`. The **exact position of the closing bracket** remains **unresolved** at the available image scale. Do not infer a complete bracket span from the unbracketed working Greek.
- **1:30, printed p. 3, middle lines:** the page visibly contains two apparent `[τοῖς]` supplements after `πᾶσι`, `[καὶ π]αντὶ`, `ἕρπον[τι ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς]`, and `[ψυχὴν]`. Diacritics, commas, and even letter boundaries still require magnified independent verification.
- **1:30, printed p. 3, lower section:** the overlooked, longer partial-word supplement appears as `χόρ[τον χλωρὸν εἰς βρῶσιν. καὶ ἐγένετο] οὕτως`. On this visual pass, its closing bracket appears **before** `οὕτως`, so it is **not** a confirmed bracket spanning 1:30 into 1:31. This replaces the earlier ambiguous 1:30–31 landmark; still a provisional reading rather than a certified diplomatic transcript.
- **1:31, printed p. 3, next lines:** observed candidate supplements remain `[καὶ ἴδεν ὁ]`, `ἐποίη[σεν, καὶ]`, `ἐγ[ένετο ἑσ]πέρα`, and `[ἡμέρα ἕ]κτη`. Exact orthography, diacritics, every printed square bracket and the tiny end-of-verse symbol await independent high-resolution check.
- **Apparatus:** the page's printed note says `29—II 3 quae uncis incl sunt perier in A` (material inside brackets perished in Codex Alexandrinus). This affects **Genesis 2:1–3** as well; it must not be interpreted as the total absence of these verses from all witnesses.

**Targeted meaning cross-check against the saved English (not a final independent accuracy pass):** The Greek `δέδωκα` in 1:29 supports the giving of plants for food; English “I give” in 1:30 makes the ellipsis intelligible. The English “behold” in 1:31 corresponds to `ἰδού`. No further English wording correction is justified *by these partial bracket observations alone*. The English chapter's draft flags remain unchanged. **New evidence:** page-3 longer partial-word bracket span plus the clarified boundary before `οὕτως`. **Still open:** precise 1:29 bracket closing, complete bracket proofread and any bracket spans not yet mapped, second Greek-English review, and qualified human sign-off.
