### Targeted follow-up: verse 29–31 original-image check (2026-11-12)

**Source actually re-opened:** the photographed [1887 printed page 3](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0031=3.png), checked against its [scan landing page](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/Page_3.html), plus [printed page 2](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0030=2.png) for the opening of verse 29. See the dated correction below. Full independently certified punctuation/diacritic check remains open.
- **1:30, printed p. 3, middle lines:** on reinspection, the earlier whole-word brackets **`[τοῖς]`** and **`[ψυχὴν]`** appear to include too many characters: the print instead reads candidate **`[τοῖ]ς`** (twice) and **`[ψυχὴ]ν`**; `[καὶ π]αντὶ` and `ἕρπον[τι ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς]` remain visible. On this visual pass, its closing bracket appears **before** `οὕτως`, so it is **not** a confirmed bracket spanning 1:30 into 1:31. - **1:31, printed p. 3, next lines:** observed candidate supplements remain `[καὶ ἴδεν ὁ]`, `ἐποίη[σεν, καὶ]`, `ἐγ[ένετο ἑσ]πέρα`, and `[ἡμέρα ἕ]κτη`. Exact orthography, diacritics, every printed square bracket and the tiny end-of-verse symbol await independent high-resolution check.

**Targeted meaning cross-check against the saved English (not an independently certified original-Greek accuracy pass):** The Greek `δέδωκα` in 1:29 supports the giving of plants for food; English “I give” in 1:30 makes the ellipsis intelligible. The English “behold” in 1:31 corresponds to `ἰδού`. No further English wording correction is justified *by these partial bracket observations alone*. The English chapter's draft flags remain unchanged. **New evidence:** page-3 longer partial-word restoration and an unresolved boundary around `καὶ ἐγένετο`; the earlier asserted single closing bracket before `οὕτως` is **withdrawn**, not confirmed. **Still open:** precise 1:29 bracket closing, the full set of distinct 1:30 brackets, all bracket and character-level proofreading, a separate independently qualified Greek-English review, and publication sign-off.

### 2026-11-12 — bracket-boundary corrections from a renewed original-print visual comparison

A renewed letter-boundary comparison was made using the actual 1887 printed [p. 2](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0030=2.png) and [p. 3](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0031=3.png). As an **independent comparator only**, a separate [2015 digital transcription attributed to Swete](https://beta.perseus.tufts.edu/urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0527.tlg001.1st1K-grc1:1/) was consulted to flag possible optical errors. That digital text is identified as deriving from a **1901 edition** and demonstrably contains transcription errors; **it is NOT our governing first-edition text and no electronic Greek was imported from it**. All promoted observations below were checked against the primary page images, not accepted on the comparator's authority.

- **1:21:** printed p. 2 supports **`[τὰ με]γάλα`**, not our earlier `[τὰ μεγάλα]`; the final `γάλα` appears outside the bracket. Additional intra-word bracket candidates in `ἑρπετῶν` and `πτερωτόν` are recorded above.
- **1:22:** printed p. 2 supports **`ὁ θ[εὸς λέγων]`**, not `ὁ [θεὸς λέγων]`; and **`πετε[ινὰ πληθυ]νέσθωσαν`** was previously omitted from the map.
- **1:24–25:** bracket candidates at the ends of `Ἐξαγαγ...`, `θηρί...`, `κτ...`, and `γέ...` now have narrower proposed start/end positions. Those require a high-resolution character-level audit before becoming source text.
- **1:29:** printed p. 3 shows the short restoration **`[ὑ]μῖν`**, **not** an unclosed bracket over the entire phrase `ὑμῖν ἔσται εἰς βρῶσιν`. This resolves the **scope of that previously misinterpreted opening** at the working-observation level; it does not prove the whole 1:29 verse's Greek diplomatic source.
- **1:30:** two apparent **`[τοῖ]ς`** and one **`[ψυχὴ]ν`** similarly have word-ending letters outside the brackets. A different ambiguous bracket group around `χόρ...` and `ἐγένετο` remains **unresolved** as the prior correction explains.
- **1:23:** a bracket at `ἐγένετο` straddles a printed line, and its exact boundary remains **uncertain**, despite the comparator's proposed reconstruction. No invented span is inserted into a canonical Greek source.

**Newly strengthened evidence is an AI visual recheck, not a qualified independent transcription.** No verse is marked certified, and all accents, apparatus variants, and remaining ambiguous brackets stay open. Retain the original note history below for traceability.

### Direct p. 2 photograph: corrected bracket interval crossing Genesis 1:22–23 (2026-11-12)

A closer visual pass over the **original Swete 1887 printed [p. 2 image](https://ccel.org/ccel/swete/lxx1/lxx1/png/0030=2.png)** shows **two distinct restoration intervals**, not the one broad restored evening/morning clause that an earlier map hypothesized:

- The end of verse 22 reads approximately `πετε[ινὰ πληθυ]νέσθωσαν ἐπὶ τῆς [γῆς.`, and the **same last bracket continues past the raised “23” verse number**, closing inside the first `ἐγένετο` at `καὶ ἐγ]ένετο`.
- The next `ἑσπέρα καὶ` is **not enclosed in that editorial bracket**. A **separate** later bracket appears in `καὶ ἐγ[ένετο πρωί]`, where the original page **breaks the word `ἐγένετο` across two printed lines** using a line-end hyphen, so neither the hyphen nor the printed verse numeral is a normal Greek word character.

The formerly logged bracket `ἐγ[ένετο ἑσπέρα καὶ ἐγένετο πρωί]` is **withdrawn as inaccurate**. The corrected candidate brackets have been checked visually against the primary 1887 p. 2 photograph. **Not yet approved as diplomatic transcription:** all individual letters, accents, the physical printed line/column positions, marginal manuscript apparatus and source cross-check need a separate exhaustive pass. This does not change the English fifth-day summary; it corrects **which portions are editorially supplied where A is damaged**.
